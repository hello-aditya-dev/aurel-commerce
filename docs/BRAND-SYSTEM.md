# AUREL — Brand Identity System

## 01 Brand Idea

AUREL is clinical skincare for stressed modern skin.

The brand exists at the intersection of clinical formulation and editorial beauty — barrier-first, evidence-led, restrained in its routines and its visual language.

## 02 Positioning

Clinical actives. Botanical intelligence. Skin, restored.

AUREL does not compete on novelty or trend. It competes on coherence: a system of products that work together, formulated around the skin barrier, delivered through an experience that feels considered and calm.

## 03 Brand Principles

1. **Barrier-first** — every formulation begins with the lipid matrix
2. **Evidence-led** — actives at meaningful concentrations, not marketing tokens
3. **Restrained** — fewer products, used consistently, not excess
4. **Tactile** — sensory experience matters; formulations should feel good
5. **Honest** — claims are specific, not inflated

## 04 Primary Wordmark

The AUREL wordmark is set in Fraunces (or a comparable editorial serif) with wide letter-spacing. It is always uppercase.

Usage:
- Near-black (`#1a1815`) on bone (`#f4f0e8`)
- Bone on near-black
- Pure black on white
- White on black

Minimum height: 16px digital, 8mm print.

## 05 Monogram

The AUREL monogram is a geometric "A" — an architectural form with a horizontal crossbar. It functions as:

- Favicon (16×16 to 512×512)
- Social avatar
- Packaging detail
- App icon

The monogram uses the same colour system as the wordmark.

## 06 Logo Usage

- The wordmark and monogram may be used independently
- They should never be stretched, rotated, or recoloured outside the palette
- Minimum clearspace: the height of the "A" cap on all sides
- Do not place on busy photographic backgrounds without a solid overlay

## 07 Clearspace

Minimum clearspace around the wordmark equals the cap height of the letter "A". No other element should enter this zone.

## 08 Minimum Sizes

- Wordmark: 16px digital / 8mm print
- Monogram: 12px digital / 6mm print

Below these sizes, use the monogram only.

## 10 Typography

| Role | Family | Usage |
|------|--------|-------|
| Display serif | Fraunces | Hero headlines, product names, editorial titles |
| Grotesk | Inter | Body, UI, commerce metadata, navigation |
| Mono | JetBrains Mono | Technical metadata, product codes, timestamps |

Type scale:
- Display: `clamp(2.75rem, 8vw, 7.5rem)` — hero only
- H1: `clamp(2.25rem, 5vw, 4.5rem)` — page openers
- H2: `clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.5rem)` — section headings
- H3: `clamp(1.25rem, 2vw, 1.5rem)` — subsections
- Body: `1rem` — standard text
- Eyebrow: `0.6875rem` + `0.22em` tracking, uppercase
- Mono: `0.6875rem` + `0.06em` tracking, uppercase

Italics: Fraunces italic is used for emphasis and editorial contrast — never for entire paragraphs.

## 12 Graphic Language

Recurring brand devices:
- **Hairline rules** — 1px borders in `#d4cec0`
- **Product numbers** — AR-0X format, JetBrains Mono
- **Oversized serif headlines** — Fraunces, light weight, tight tracking
- **Editorial grids** — 12-column, deliberate asymmetry
- **Mineral backgrounds** — warm bone, never pure white
- **Mono metadata strips** — technical context without clutter

## 13 Photography

### Product Photography
- Soft directional studio light
- Warm bone/mineral environment
- Controlled reflections
- Sharp packaging, no text artifacts
- Quiet, centered composition

### Ingredient Photography
- Macro scale
- Scientific aesthetic
- Glass, liquid, mineral, botanical structures
- Warm bone background

### Human Photography
- Realistic skin texture
- Editorial crop
- Minimal retouching
- Quiet emotion
- Not influencer photography

### Campaign Photography
- Stone, water, glass, shadow, skin, product
- Museum-like composition
- Vast negative space
- Warm directional light

## 14 Packaging

Every AUREL package follows the same hierarchy:

```
AUREL

AR-0X

PRODUCT NAME

(optional key active)

VOLUME
```

Materials:
- Frosted smoked glass (serums, jars)
- Brushed metal caps/lids
- Bone-coloured labels
- Matte black droppers
- Soft-touch tubes (SPF)

The packaging family must look like eight products from one formulation lab — not eight individually generated images.

## 15 Tone of Voice

AUREL sounds:
- Precise
- Calm
- Intelligent
- Restrained
- Specific
- Credible

AUREL avoids:
- "unlock"
- "revolutionary"
- "game-changing"
- "transform your skin"
- "luxurious"
- "premium"
- "best-ever"
- Generic beauty fluff

The presentation should communicate quality. AUREL should rarely need to call itself luxurious.

## 16 Digital Application

- Wordmark in header: live typography (Fraunces), not SVG — crisper at all sizes
- Monogram in favicon: SVG
- Product numbers: JetBrains Mono, uppercase
- Commerce metadata: Inter, tabular-nums
- Editorial headlines: Fraunces, light weight, fluid clamp sizing
- Eyebrows: Inter, 0.6875rem, 0.22em tracking, uppercase

## 17 Social Identity

- Default OG image: 1200×630, composited with AUREL wordmark + tagline + product
- Case study OG: 1200×630, dark background, different copy
- Social avatar: AUREL monogram on dark background
- All social assets use the canonical colour palette
- No platform-specific logos or badges in OG images

## 18 Incorrect Usage Examples

- Do not stretch or distort the wordmark
- Do not recolour outside the palette
- Do not place the wordmark on busy backgrounds without overlay
- Do not use more than one accent colour at a time
- Do not use "luxurious," "revolutionary," or "game-changing"
- Do not present fictional claims as factual medical evidence
- Do not watermark editorial photography
